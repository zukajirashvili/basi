Delete your account · BUS (ბასი)

In the app

  1. Open BUS.
  2. In the bottom menu, tap პროფილი (Profile).
  3. Tap ანგარიშის წაშლა (Delete account) and confirm.

If you signed in a long time ago, the app asks you to sign out and back in first, for security.

Without the app

Email lunaviolab@gmail.com and tell us the phone number or email you signed up with. We delete the account within 7 days and confirm when it's done.

What gets deleted

Your account (phone number or email, and password), your name, settings, saved route, driver route and times, the marshrutka's last location, and any ride record. We keep none of this data after deletion.
